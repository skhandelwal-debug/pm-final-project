# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Frontline Driver
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Finish the route quickly, logging each delivery in seconds and without backtracking.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Marking a stop delivered takes 3 taps across 3 screens at the doorstep (UXR-01, BUG-2055). Mid-route the app can crash and lose the remaining stops (UXR-03, BUG-2031), or go blank offline (UXR-06, BUG-2050). The moment of misery is when the driver gives up on the app and texts the dispatcher, calls the office, or uses a paper manifest. 5 of 7 drivers in the focus group already carry one (UXR-12).
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Text, Paper manifest, phone call
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Texting the dispatcher instead of marking a stop delivered, Calling the office after a crash, Carrying a paper manifest or a screenshot as backup, Retaking proof-of-delivery photos up to 3 times
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Overriding the suggested route every day
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** it ignores closed roads and loading docks (UXR-05, BUG-2068)
