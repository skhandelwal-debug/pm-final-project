1. Chosen scenario

Path: RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

I picked it because it is an enterprise platform where power has outgrown everyday usefulness, and the challenge is to simplify the daily experience while keeping high-end enterprise capabilities available.

2. The strategic crisis

If we do nothing for 12 months, RouteLogic risks being known as too complex for the work it was built to speed up. Our largest enterprise account is already trialing a leaner competitor, and usage among fleet coordinators is falling. Churn could follow, putting our revenue and reputation at risk.

3. The moment of misery

Hypothesis to validate in discovery. This is inferred and is not stated in the brief.

The fleet coordinator is likely running time-sensitive work outside RouteLogic, in spreadsheets, messages or phone calls. These workarounds may bypass our built-in compliance controls, and admins may not know it is happening.

4. Problem hook

We must protect our largest enterprise account and our market reputation by addressing the friction that fleet coordinators face in their most time-sensitive work.

5. Value proposition

For fleet coordinators, we will make real-time coordination fast and simple, while high-end enterprise capabilities enable organization-specific guardrails, because our largest account is already trialing a leaner competitor.
